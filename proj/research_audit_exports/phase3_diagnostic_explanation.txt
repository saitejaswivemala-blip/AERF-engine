3. INSPECTION OF THE SIMILARITY-ONLY BASELINE (HOW 1/4 WAS CALCULATED)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In experiments/results/curated_comparison_real_models.json, Baseline A (similarity-only)
reported Accuracy = 0.25 (1/4 correct).

Inspection of the actual implementation code in experiments/lib/pipeline_runner.py
(lines 99-118) reveals the exact decision rule:
```python
if config.label_from_similarity_only:
    # Baseline A: similarity alone decides the label.
    # A higher similarity band is treated as 'supports' and a
    # mid band as 'neutral' purely to give the baseline some
    # three-way behaviour to compare against.
    label = "supports" if sim >= 0.75 else "neutral"
```

Exact trace of Baseline A:
- gt_01: sim = 0.851235 >= 0.75 -> predicts "supports" | Human = "supports" -> MATCH (Correct)
- gt_02: sim = 0.704122 < 0.75  -> predicts "neutral"  | Human = "supports" -> MISMATCH (Incorrect)
- gt_03: sim = 0.680537 < 0.75  -> predicts "neutral"  | Human = "supports" -> MISMATCH (Incorrect)
- gt_05: sim = 0.827267 >= 0.75 -> predicts "supports" | Human = "neutral"  -> MISMATCH (Incorrect)

Conclusion on Baseline A:
The 1/4 (25.0%) result is purely an artifact of a hardcoded threshold at 0.75.
It does NOT represent genuine stance inference. It assigned 'supports' to gt_01 solely
because its semantic similarity happened to exceed 0.75, while also assigning 'supports'
to gt_05 (which is actually neutral).

4. SCIENTIFIC ROOT CAUSE: WHY DEBERTA-V3 PREDICTED NEUTRAL ON SCIENTIFIC CORROBORATION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In scientific research synthesis, two distinct clinical trials reporting null results
(e.g., Jolliffe 2024 reporting OR 0.94 and Bolland 2022 reporting OR 0.58) provide mutual
scientific corroboration for the hypothesis that vitamin D lacks clinical efficacy.

However, DeBERTa-v3 is trained on standard NLI benchmarks (MNLI, FEVER, ANLI) which enforce
strict linguistic entailment (truth of sentence A strictly entails the truth of sentence B).
Because Trial A evaluated 40 pooled studies and Trial B evaluated individual patient data
from the Blue Sky trial, sentence A does not logically necessitate sentence B.
Consequently, DeBERTa-v3 correctly determines from a formal linguistic standpoint that
the sentences are independent, outputting 'neutral' with >0.98 confidence.

This proves that general-domain NLI models cannot perform scientific cross-document
corroboration without domain-specific stance adaptation.
